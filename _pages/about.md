Personal Details
======
I am **Junteng Liu**, a **first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group**, which is a natural language processing research group at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)** led by **Professor Junxian He**. I am supervised by Professor Junxian He, who also previously advised me during my undergraduate studies at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)**, from which I graduated in **June 2024**. My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**.

Research Interests
======
My research focuses on **Natural Language Processing** (my primary research focus) combined with **Machine Learning**. Within natural language processing, my research interests include:

* **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
* **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)** — focusing on VLM hallucination problems
* **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability** — focusing on the truthfulness and interpretability aspects of large language models
